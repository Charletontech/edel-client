Our Mission

The local marketplace for trusted services.

E-del connects customers with nearby service providers through clear pricing, location-aware discovery, and verified service profiles.

The E-del Vision

E-del is a web-based service marketplace that connects customers with service providers in their local area. We believe that finding reliable help should be simple, not stressful.

Our platform is built to be location-aware, surfacing relevant services and requests based on proximity. Whether you're looking for a deep home clean or offering emergency plumbing, E-del keeps the connection clear, verified, and practical.

Built on Trust

Trust is the foundation of the local economy. That's why we've implemented the Service Start Verification System. By using QR codes and precise geolocation, we confirm that a provider has physically arrived at a customer's location before any job begins.

This QR handshake helps prevent confusion and gives both parties a clear start point for every service.

Scalable Innovation

Starting as a cash-first MVP, E-del is designed to scale. We are building toward a more complete service marketplace with stronger payments, better discovery, and richer provider tools over time.

At a Glance

  • Proximity-First: Real-time discovery based on your GPS location.
  • Verified Starts: QR handshake ensures physical arrival.
  • Tiered Growth: Users progress through Rookie, Veteran, and Elite tiers.
  • Fair Ratings: Dynamic rating system for both parties.
