Our Mission

The global standard for local services.

Edel bridges the gap between your needs and local talent. We’re building a world where professional services are as accessible and secure as your favorite banking app.

The Edel Vision

Edel is a web-based service marketplace platform that connects customers with service providers in their local area. We believe that physical proximity shouldn't be the only barrier to finding high-quality help.

Our platform is built to be location-aware, surfacing relevant services and requests based on proximity. Whether you're looking for a deep home clean or offering emergency plumbing, Edel ensures the connection is seamless, verified, and professional.

Built on Trust

Trust is the foundation of the local economy. That's why we've implemented the Service Start Verification System. By using cryptographically secure QR codes and precise geolocation, we confirm that a provider has physically arrived at a customer's location before any job begins.

This "QR Handshake" prevents fraud and ensures both parties are protected, creating a safe environment for everyone in the community.

Scalable Innovation

Starting as a cash-first MVP, Edel is designed to scale. We are committed to evolving into a fully integrated payment and logistics ecosystem, making the business of local services more efficient and rewarding for all.

At a Glance

  • Proximity-First: Real-time discovery based on your GPS location.
  • Verified Starts: QR Handshake ensures physical arrival.
  • Tiered Growth: Users progress through Rookie, Veteran, and Elite tiers.
  • Fair Ratings: Dynamic rating system for both parties.
