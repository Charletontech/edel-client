Growth

Your skills. Your business.

Turn your local talent into a professional service business. Edel provides the tools to get discovered, manage requests, and build a lasting reputation.

Get Discovered

On Edel, your visibility is tied to your location. When customers search for services, we surface the best providers closest to them. Every listing card features your profile photo, service title, pricing, and your verified rating.

Service Portfolio

You can list a maximum of 5 independent services under a single provider account. Whether you offer home cleaning, electrical repairs, and tutoring, you can manage them all from one professional profile.

Build Your Reputation

Your rating starts at 50% and increases with every successfully completed order. Higher ratings lead to better visibility and more trust from potential customers.

Rating Logic

Your score goes up when jobs are completed without reports. It may decrease if disputes are filed or orders are cancelled without valid reason.

Tier Badges

All new providers start as Rookie (Blue Tick). As you grow, you'll unlock Veteran, Elite, and Platinum tiers with exclusive benefits.

Control Your Schedule

You're the boss. Toggle your availability status instantly between Available and Unavailable. When you're on a job, the system automatically sets you to Busy, so you can focus on the customer at hand without distractions.

Provider Checklist

  • Profile Photo: A clear, professional photo increases bookings.
  • Pricing: Set flat rates or "Starting from" prices.
  • Location: Enable GPS so nearby customers can find you.
  • Verification: Be ready to scan QR codes on arrival.
