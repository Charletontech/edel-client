The Journey

How Edel Works.

From finding the right professional to completing a job securely, discover the step-by-step flow that makes Edel unique.

For Customers

1. Discover

Browse trending services nearby. We show you the closest pros first, along with their ratings and tier badges.

2. Order

Found what you need? Tap 'Order' to send a real-time request. You'll be notified as soon as the provider accepts.

3. Handshake

When the pro arrives, generate your unique QR code. Once they scan it, the service officially begins.

For Providers

1. Accept

Receive instant notifications for requests in your area. Review the customer details and accept the job.

2. Travel

Head to the customer's location. Your status automatically switches to 'Busy' while the order is active.

3. Verify

Scan the customer's QR code on arrival. Our system verifies your proximity before marking the service as started.

The Service Verification System

We've replaced manual check-ins with a high-security verification flow. This ensures that every job logged on Edel is authentic and performed on-site.

How it stays secure:

  • Single-Use Tokens: Every QR code is unique and expires in 3 minutes.
  • GPS Precision: We compare both parties' locations with a 50-meter accuracy requirement.
  • Socket.io Events: Status updates happen in real-time, instantly notifying both apps.
