Provider Agreement.

This agreement outlines the professional standards and obligations for service providers on the Edel platform.

1. Service Standards

As a provider, you agree to maintain high standards of professionalism and quality. You must accurately describe your services, set fair prices, and keep your availability status up to date.

Service Limit

Each provider account is limited to a maximum of 5 unique service listings. This ensures quality and focus within your profile.

2. Verification & QR Handshake

You agree to comply with our QR Handshake verification flow. This means you MUST scan the customer's unique QR code upon physical arrival at the job site. Failure to follow this flow may lead to jobs not being recorded, loss of tier status, or account suspension.

Geolocation

You must have GPS enabled during active orders. We verify your location against the customer's to ensure the service is performed where requested.

3. Fees & Billing

The first 3 successfully completed orders on Edel are free of platform fees. To continue receiving requests after the 3rd order, you must pay a one-time, non-refundable Access Fee of ₦3,500 via Paystack.

This fee grants you permanent access to the platform without monthly subscriptions or commissions on your service earnings.

4. Conduct & Termination

Misuse of the platform, including fraudulent reviews, off-platform solicitations to bypass safety protocols, or consistent negative reports, will result in immediate termination of access without refund of the Access Fee.

Tier Progression

  • Rookie: 0-10 Completed Orders
  • Veteran: 11-50 Completed Orders
  • Elite: 51-200 Completed Orders
  • Platinum: 200+ Completed Orders
